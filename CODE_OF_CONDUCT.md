# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Limits Apply welcoming and harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of expertise. Scientific
disagreement is welcome; personal attacks are not.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, respectful, and evidence-led.
- Critique claims and methods rather than people.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Respect privacy, provider terms, and the limits of submitted data.
- Accept corrections and distinguish uncertainty from misconduct.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate disclosure of
private information, sustained disruption, and retaliation against good-faith reports are not
tolerated in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings, impose temporary
restrictions, or permanently exclude participants when necessary. Decisions should be proportionate,
documented privately, and protect reporters from retaliation.

Do not put a conduct report in a public issue. A verified private conduct-reporting address must be
published here before the repository is opened to public contributions. Until then, the project is
not operationally ready to accept confidential conduct reports. Reports will be handled
confidentially to the extent possible and must protect reporters from retaliation.

This policy is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
