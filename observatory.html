Community observations

What one quota point bought, measured on a real subscription.

The Historical Quota Observatory is the community's half of this project. Contributors collect quota intervals on their own accounts — X tokens of a known mix consumed Y points of quota in one window — and the page reads the aggregation of what they sent, one JSON record per interval. Nothing here is modelled from a plan's price.

Every figure carries the status of the sample behind it. An interval set too thin to estimate says insufficient data, and that is a valid answer: it is what the page shows until enough people report the same plan, model and window.

One interval is a report, never an estimate.

Fewer than five stay that way; three reporters make it a community figure.

01 — the store

One plan, one model, one window at a time.

A group is a plan, a model, a window type and a harness — nothing wider. A combination no interval has been collected for cannot be selected, so the selects never offer a plan, a model or a window this store has never seen.

02 — the evidence

What this sample is allowed to claim.

Intervals and the distinct reporters behind them, the window they were observed over, and the three figures the project exists to measure: tokens per quota point, the API-equivalent dollars per quota point at a published rate card, and the share of those tokens that arrived from cache.

03 — the history

Every interval, and the ones that were refused.

One row per admissible interval, oldest first, and the priced series drawn as a line. An interval that failed admissibility keeps its identifier and the reason it was refused — provenance, never statistics.

04 — the export

Take the selection with you.

Both exports are cut from the selection above, in this browser. Nothing is uploaded, and neither file claims a figure the rows behind it do not carry.

05 — provenance

Where the file comes from, and how to read one record.

This page reads one generated file and nothing else. A single observation — every field, the admissibility rules and the validation that refuses a malformed one — is published as the observations schema, next to the code that enforces it.
