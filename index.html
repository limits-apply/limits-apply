How much work does your AI plan actually buy?

Find the best AI subscription as models, prices and limits change. We keep track so you can see which plans offer the strongest models and how much work your monthly budget could buy.

Every provider counts usage differently. We use Artificial Analysis benchmarks to put plans on a common scale: model capability and estimated monthly capacity, based on published allowances and the tokens each model needs for the same tasks.

These figures are theoretical ceilings, with the source and calculation shown for each plan. Your actual usage will vary.
