01 — the scenarios

At a fixed budget, the options that are actually available.

A single subscription, the vendor’s next tier, a paid top-up where the provider offers one, a second independent provider, or team seats the vendor explicitly authorises. Stacking accounts of the same plan to beat a personal cap is not one of them.

02 — the terms

Why the cheapest multiplier is not on the list.

Several accounts of one plan multiply the arithmetic cleanly and breach the published terms. The simulator keeps them in an analytical register with their status and the clause that decides it, and never exports them as a configuration.
