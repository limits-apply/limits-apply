Compare free AI tools by model access, features, and usage limits — not by premium benchmark scores alone. Every free row is $0, names the model it actually serves you, and says when nobody has published the cap.

01 — the catalogue

Filter by what you actually want to do.

A free tier is not one offer: the model you reach by default, the one you can select, and the one it falls back to are often three different things. Filter by usage and region, then read the model, the limits and the fallbacks together — an unquantified cap stays labelled unknown rather than filled in.

02 — the method

Why there is no single “best free AI” score.

Quality × capacity ÷ price is undefined at a price of zero, and any substitute would invent the number the free tier does not publish. So the page shows categories and compromises instead: the best free option for coding, for search, for reasoning, for long documents. A row is only listed against a model when a source shows that offer actually serving it.
